Glossary

The words on the forms, in the order a person meets them.

Articles of Organization
The formation filing for a limited liability company. It is filed with the state, and the state's filed copy is the entity's proof of formation.
EIN
The employer identification number. The IRS issues it to an entity for tax, banking and payroll use. It is not a password, and it is not secret in the way a bank login is.
Registered agent
The person or company with an address in the state that receives legal papers and state notices for an entity.
Good standing
The state's record that an entity has filed its reports and paid its state fees. A state can show this status on request.
Compliance calendar
The list of recurring due dates for one entity: annual reports, agent changes and any other filing the state requires.
Principal
The person or agent that asked for a filing. Each filing names its principal in the entity record.
Quote
The price for one job, shown before you hire it. Charter House prices each job on request and shows the state fees apart from its own fee.
